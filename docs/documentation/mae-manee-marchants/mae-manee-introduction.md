# Mae Manee App Introduction

Mae Manee App is a small SME (sSME) merchant application that accepts a variety of payment types, including QR tag 30, QR Card Schema, Alipay/WeChatPay, Payment Link, and other digital payments in the future.

Using the Mae Manee App, merchants will be able to better manage their own businesses. With a comprehensive service that addresses all of the most common business issues, including the following highlighted services:  

1.  Receive money simply Merchants can receive money from their customers easily via QR code or Payment Link. They no longer have to prepare for cash and deal with the issue of not having enough change for their customers.
    
2.  The staff can assist with payment acceptance Merchants can let their staff accept payments while they are away by adding their mobile numbers to the shop account on the Mae Manee App.
    
3.  Clearly separate the sales With their shop's daily sales summary, merchants can keep their personal and business transactions separate on the Mae Manee App.
    
4.  Get instant notifications Merchants can receive immediate notifications for any payment accepted by themselves or their shop staff.
    

**Note:**  
\- Merchants must have the SCB EASY App in order to register for the Mae Manee App.
